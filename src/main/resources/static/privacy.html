SMACian Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, date of birth, gender, email/phone, and profile photos.
  • Authentication Data: Password (stored as a secure one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Usage Data: Content you post and interactions within the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To authenticate your login
  • To display your profile to other users
  • To secure your account and detect fraud

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Passwords are stored using BCrypt hashing - we cannot read your password.
  • Photos are stored on secure cloud servers.
  • We use HTTPS encryption (JWT tokens) for all communications.

4. Photo Storage

Profile and cover photos are stored using Cloudinary cloud storage. By uploading photos, you consent to this processing.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting the admin.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to users.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact our admin team.
